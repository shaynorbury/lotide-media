Offline conversions

Automation

Speed to lead, measured in seconds.

The first business to respond usually wins. Here's what happens the moment a lead comes in, day or night.

0sForm submitted

Quiz answers and source captured, with click IDs.

2sScored

Lead scored against your intake rules and past clients.

5sRouted

Sent to the right person by score, region and hours.

60sLogged

CRM record, task and alert created. The clock is visible to your team.

What we count

Four numbers, defined once.

Qualified lead

Meets your intake rules: the right need, the right size, a real way to reach them.

Booked consult

A qualified lead who scheduled time with your team.

Signed client

A consult that became a paying client, confirmed in your CRM.

Cost per client

Total ad spend and fees divided by signed clients. The number every decision answers to.
